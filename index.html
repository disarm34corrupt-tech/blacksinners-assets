Beauty is dangerous. Kindness is persuasive. Darkness is irresistible.

At Blacksinners, angels and devils are not enemies—Angels embrace their Devils, and Devils remember their Light. They are reflections of the same soul. Beauty conceals danger. Kindness masks deception. Every smile hides a temptation, and every shadow longs to be understood.

But this month, one of us is not what they seem.

Here, every member is a suspect.

THE GAME · 4 WEEKS

WHO'S THE DEMON?

Siapa demon-nya?

A Demon is hiding among us, disguised as an ordinary member. Every member's task is to collect clues and work out who the Demon is before the event ends. The Demon's task is to survive without being found out.

The Flow

  1. I–IIWEEKS

    Introductions & Clue Gathering

    • Member introductions: every participant introduces themselves and picks or receives a character (name, traits, signature quirk).
    • Weekly games earn clues. Clues at this stage are general.
      1MOD
      1Vote #3
      2Vote #2
      3Vote #1
    • Participants keep their own record of every clue they receive.
  2. IIIWEEK

    The Demon Is Chosen

    • The admin secretly appoints the Demon by private message.
    • The Demon receives its role and special rules (see Roles).
    • The admin starts releasing more specific clues. Some are true, some may be decoys.
    • Members begin investigating and suspecting each other (keep it healthy!). Discussion is allowed, but never repeat the admin's original clue word for word.
  3. IVWEEK

    The Hunt & The Closing

    • Investigation: participants analyse every clue they hold. Discussion is closed.
    • Voting, Thursday: each participant privately sends the admin their guess, who the Demon is and why.
    • Reveal, Saturday or just before the agency closes: the admin unmasks the Demon, names the winners and closes the event.

Finding the Demon

Like a game of undercover, the Demon never stands out. Clues arrive indirectly:

Descriptive
General traits that could fit several people. "The demon likes to be active at night."
Behavioural
Habits you can observe during the event. "The demon often answers last."
Word / Code
The Demon receives a word slightly different from everyone else's, so its answers may look a little off.
Decoy
The admin slips in false clues to keep the game interesting.

Members reach a conclusion by matching all the clues together, never by guessing at random.

Rules of Play

  1. Every participant must complete the weekly missions before the deadline.
  2. Do not leak the admin's private clues raw. You may discuss them in your own words.
  3. The Demon may not confess before Week 4.
  4. Each participant may send only one guess, during Week 4.
  5. Respect every participant. Accusing is allowed; personal attacks are not.
  6. The admin's decision is final.

Judgement

  • Members win by guessing the Demon correctly, with bonus points for the most logical reasoning.
  • The Demon wins if no one, or only a few, guess correctly.
  • Winners may receive a prize.
